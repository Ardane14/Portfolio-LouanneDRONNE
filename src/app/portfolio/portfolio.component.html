Humus is a contemporary tabletop role-playing game set in a world where humans possess supernatural abilities, and hero schools and media coverage coexist. Built around a custom skill-based system, the game emphasizes narrative freedom, character development, and player agency. The project reflects a strong focus on storytelling, world coherence, and systemic design rooted in narrative gameplay.

The Jasper Crown is an original fantasy novel built around a richly developed world and a carefully structured narrative. The project focuses on deep worldbuilding, intertwining political tensions, fractured empires, and individual destinies within a dark fantasy universe. Through a character-driven story, the book explores themes of power, downfall, loyalty, and identity.

ISALIVE is a narrative physical escape game where players interact with an AI named AIDEN. Designed to question AI ethics and technological dependence, the experience offers three endings shaped by player choices. I worked on the story, gameplay mechanics, and AI programming.

ATUA is a fictional interactive performance project exploring the relationship between live movement and generative visuals. Designed as a stage experience, dancers interact with real-time graphic elements through motion tracking and interactive systems. Although never staged, the project served as a research and experimentation space using tools such as TouchDesigner, Kinect, and Processing to explore embodied interaction and digital scenography.
